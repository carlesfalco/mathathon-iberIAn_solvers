## What the columns mean

"Matched" means one of three things, named per model: **exact solution** = a closed-form solution of the continuous problem, so agreement is limited by the discretization and the tolerance is the documented discretization level, not ε; **invariant** = an identity that must hold (FFT identity and entry count, Parseval, Rayleigh expansion); **published behaviour** = a qualitative or scaling result (bounded tile rank). No row reproduces a table from a paper number for number.

*dial lines* = code that plugs the physics into the harness (kernel, sampler / proxy override, wrappers, forked driver); *physics lines* = formulation, quadrature, geometry and analytic reference. Both are counted automatically with inspect.getsource (non-blank, non-comment, docstrings excluded). `unified/core` and `unified/drivers` are imported unchanged by every model. *error vs analytic* is the fast solution against the reference, with the dense solution's error in brackets: the gap between the two is what the fast solver adds. *fast vs dense* is ‖x_fast − x_dense‖/‖x_dense‖ (solves) or the same for outputs (transforms). *time* is fast / dense wall seconds on one laptop (secondary evidence; drivers keep dense work arrays).
